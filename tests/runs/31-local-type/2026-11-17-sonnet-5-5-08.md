- **Horizon**: the drink-by year. Past it, the bottle is due: drink it, or drop it.
- **Fields**: wine, vintage, rack, shelf, drink-by (year).
- **One object per bottle**, in `objects/bottles/`: each can be drunk, moved or dropped on its own. Two bottles of the same wine are two objects.
